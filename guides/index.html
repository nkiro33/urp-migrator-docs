Have more than a handful of shaders?

Let URP Migrator do the rewrite

URP Migrator scans your project, then converts surface shaders, lit vertex/fragment shaders, GrabPass and OnRenderImage effects to URP. It keeps your surf, vertex and lighting functions as they are, keeps material property names, previews every change and backs up every file. On a 52-file test corpus, 44/44 converted shaders compile on four graphics APIs and 41/44 render within 2/255 of Built-in (median 0.46/255; Unity's own Standard to URP/Lit conversion measures 0.77/255).
